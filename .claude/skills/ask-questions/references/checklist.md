# Question checklist
users and roles · main goals / success metric · each feature's steps · what happens on errors ·
data to store, who owns it, how long to keep it · integrations (payment, email, SMS, maps, SSO, calendar) ·
admin needs · reports and exports · notifications · languages and time zones · devices and browsers ·
performance and expected users · security, privacy and compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, PCI) · accessibility ·
existing systems / data migration · hosting constraints · deadlines, budget, milestones ·
who approves what · what is explicitly out of scope.
